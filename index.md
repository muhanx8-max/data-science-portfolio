## Featured Project: Mapping Poaching Incident Locations in Tanzania

**Project Status:** Completed (Course Exercise)

### Research Question

How can dot-density, spatial histogram, and Voronoi visualizations reveal geographic patterns in the recorded poaching incidents in Tanzania?

### Project Overview

This project analyzes a dataset containing 268 recorded poaching incidents in Tanzania. Each record includes information about the incident and its geographical coordinates.

Using Python and geospatial visualization techniques, I explored how the recorded incidents are distributed geographically and how different mapping approaches reveal spatial patterns.

### Methods

Three visualization techniques were used:

1. **Dot Density Map:** Displays individual poaching incident locations as geographical points.
2. **Spatial Histogram:** Uses colored grid cells to highlight areas with higher concentrations of recorded incidents.
3. **Voronoi Tessellation:** Illustrates spatial relationships and proximity between recorded locations.

![Poaching Voronoi Map](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/muhanx8-max/data-science-project/main/images/poaching_voronoi.png)

The Voronoi visualization highlights spatial proximity relationships between the recorded locations.


### Key Findings

The recorded incidents are not uniformly distributed across the study area. Some locations form visible spatial concentrations, which are easier to recognize using maps than from a table of coordinates.

Different visualization techniques provide complementary perspectives on the same dataset.

### Project Limitations

The available incident records may not represent all poaching events. Spatial clustering alone does not establish the causes of poaching activity. In addition, the older Geoplotlib library can experience compatibility problems with modern map tile services.
